How to play

There is nothing complicated to learn. Read, explore, and try things.

1

Read the page

Each place gives you part of the story. Take your time and enjoy it.

2

Tap interesting things

Flowers, books, letters, and other glowing objects may hide a clue or a puzzle.

3

Choose where to go

Use the buttons below the story to travel. Your choices can change what happens later.

Stuck? Ask the kitten.

Tap the kitten in the corner for a hint. Open Journal to see clues you have already found. You can always press Restart to begin again.

Make a story that is yours

The Creator is a simple page for changing the book. You do not need to write code—just write your story and choose from the menus.

1. Start with the Story tab

Set the book title, your friend’s name, and your name. Type {name} in your writing when you want their name to appear.

2. Write a scene

A scene is one page of the adventure. Give it a title, a short piece of story text, and choices that take the player somewhere next.

3. Add things to discover

Add an object such as a flower, book, letter, or lantern. You can also upload your own small picture for that object.

4. Make a simple puzzle

Choose Text, Choice, Sequence, or Audio Choice. You can upload a short sound clue, then write the question, answer, and kind hints.

5. Connect the story

Give a clue when the player finds something, then make a later choice or puzzle need that clue. It works just like finding a key before opening a door.

6. Publish and try it

Press Publish to the game, open Play, press Restart, and test your new part. Change anything that does not feel right.

Keep your story safe

Use Export JSON in the Creator whenever you make a change you love. It includes your uploaded pictures and sounds. Keep files small: images under 1.5 MB and sounds under 2 MB.

One good surprise is enough

Start small: a secret note, a riddle, and a warm final letter. Make it personal, test it once, and let the story grow from there.
